Our Story

Every Signature
Tells a Story.

As the story goes, back in 1993 a customer from El Paso, Texas grabbed a marker and left his mark on the shrimp truck. The crew liked it, so they let it stay. A few more signatures showed up the next day, and the tradition never stopped.

Today the truck is covered edge to edge in thousands of names from customers all over the world. It's basically a rolling guestbook of everyone who's stopped by for a plate.

Our History

From One Truck to a North Shore Icon

1993

The Beginning

Giovanni's started operating out of a converted 1953 bread truck, with just a handful of recipes and an entrepreneurial spirit driving the beloved truck around the North Shore.

1996–1997

Two Trucks, Two Towns

The truck parked for good on Kamehameha Highway in historic Kahuku. In 1997, a second truck opened in Haleiwa on what was then an overgrown field. It's now one of the busiest spots in town.

1999

National Recognition

Giovanni's began entering national and international food show contests, winning multiple awards for the Hot & Spicy sauce and the Scampi marinade.

2001

A New Truck

Word of mouth grew from locals and tourists alike, so much that the original truck was retired for a new one.

2006–2010

Putting Down Roots

Giovanni's became such a part of the community that the land the truck was parked on was purchased outright. By 2010, a pavilion was built so guests could enjoy their shrimp, the weather, and each other.
